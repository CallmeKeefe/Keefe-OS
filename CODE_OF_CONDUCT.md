# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KeefeOS aims to be a respectful, constructive space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Participation means treating other people with dignity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique technical ideas and evidence, not a person's identity or worth.
- Explain disagreements with reproducible facts where possible.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them openly.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its technical goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Knowingly presenting untested functionality as verified, especially where this could create security or data-loss risks.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/CallmeKeefe). Include relevant links and context, but do not expose sensitive personal information.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and may edit or remove content, request changes, temporarily restrict participation, or block a participant for conduct that violates this policy. Response times may vary.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communications.
