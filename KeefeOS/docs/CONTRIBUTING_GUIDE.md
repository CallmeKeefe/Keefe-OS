The AI, LLM, capability, scanner, and security modules under the kernel are experimental interfaces or deterministic demos. They are not a connected general-purpose AI agent, process-isolation system, or production security boundary. Hardware support must be claimed only for a specific configuration verified by CI or documented device testing.
